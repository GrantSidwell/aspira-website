## The building

The Cattle Market building on Wheatfield Way is a single-storey entrance pavilion to a pedestrian subway under the A307. Its plan is a keyhole: a circular glazed drum (about 9.4 m across at the wall line) with a conical roof and glazed lantern, joined to an angled wing that holds two lifts to the car park and subway, a gabled entrance porch and a small office.
